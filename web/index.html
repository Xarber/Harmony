AT HOME ON YOUR DEVICES

Choose a place to listen.

Web · ready for local use

Import, play, search, favorite, edit metadata and build playlists. Install where your browser supports it.

Open web app

On iPhone or iPad, open in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen. On supported desktop/Android browsers, use the browser’s install action. HTTPS or localhost is required.

iPhone, iPad & Mac · in development

The native SwiftUI player has an offline SQLite library and Apple media integrations. macOS playback has been tested; iPhone validation and signed distribution are still pending.

There is no published native download yet. No release link or store availability is implied.

OWNERSHIP, WITHOUT THE NOISE

Private by design. Open at heart.

Harmony has no advertising or listening analytics. The web app keeps audio on your device, and works offline after its first successful load. Keep your original files: browsers can evict local storage.
